# Corpus — canonical DOI register

The complete DOI register for the rectified-frame-potential / interpretability-metrology paper
series: 32 published papers, 21 published verification-record deposits, and the items that have
no DOI yet.

**Verification date:** 2026-08-27
**Method:** every DOI below was resolved against the live Zenodo record (`https://zenodo.org/records/<id>`)
and the live record title compared against the paper or deposit it is claimed to belong to in the
repository's own metadata files. No DOI in this file is quoted from local notes alone. Where a
local file and the live record disagreed, the live record wins and the disagreement is recorded
under **Flags** — no correction is guessed.

**Reconciliation (same date):** this register was then reconciled against a second, independently
built DOI map produced on another machine by enumerating the account's live Zenodo records. That
map surfaced several record ids not present in the series' internal records. All were fetched and
their version relationships determined from the live pages. **Every one turned out to be a Zenodo
version or concept-DOI relationship — none is a duplicate record.** The version structure is set
out in **FLAG-9**, which is the authority for which identifier to cite; Tables 1 and 2 carry the
citable identifier for each item.

## Flags

Every DOI in Tables 1 and 2 resolved live and its live title matched the paper or deposit it is
claimed to belong to. There are **no unresolved DOIs, no title mismatches, and — after the
reconciliation — no duplicate records.** The items below are version structure, gaps, and
out-of-series records that a reader of this register should know about.

**FLAG-1 — paper 21's two live records are a VERSION PAIR, not duplicates. RESOLVED.**
An earlier pass of this register found `10.5281/zenodo.21426763` and `10.5281/zenodo.21446304`
both resolving to a live preprint titled "The Self-Knowledge Map: Where Language Models Do — and
Do Not — Know What They Know", both dated 2026-07-19, and flagged them as possible duplicates
needing a ruling. The live pages settle it: **21426763 is v1** and carries the "There is a newer
version of the record available" banner; **21446304 is v2 and is the latest version.** They are
one record with two versions. Table 1 now cites the latest version. No ruling is needed and
nothing should be withdrawn. Both ids stay valid and resolvable.

file names 21426763 as the paper record (true when written — v2 did not exist yet), and one
summary document cites 21426732 as paper 21, which is live but is the paper-21
**deposit** (Table 2), not the paper.

cite — the concept DOI for "this work", the version DOI for "exactly this file". Every one of the
nine reconciliation pairs falls into this structure. Nothing here is a duplicate.

Notes on the choices above:
- Where a **concept DOI is known**, Tables 1–2 cite it. It is the identifier already printed in the
  series' own bibliographies and in the published papers' related-identifier fields, and it always
  serves the newest version — so it stays correct as further versions are published. The v1 and v2
  ids are recorded here so any specific version remains citable.
- Where **no concept DOI was determinable** from the live page, Tables 1–2 cite the **latest
  version**, and the superseded v1 id is recorded here. The concept DOIs for those five items
  almost certainly exist; they were not exposed on the fetched pages and are marked "not
  determined" rather than guessed.
- **v2 substance, where the live record states it:** paper 1 v2 adds the unconditional two-point
  corollary and the amended conjecture; papers 2 and 3 v2 carry the corrected equation (3) and the
  sign-rigidity theorem respectively; deposit CL v2 "adds two JSON artifacts omitted from v1"; the
  qLDPC v3 adds the verification package. Papers 19, 21, and 30 v2 revisions are not characterized
  on their record pages.
- **Consequence for outreach and CVs:** anything that cites paper 19, 21, 30, deposit CL, or the
  qLDPC preprint by its v1 id is pointing at a superseded version. The link still resolves and
  shows a newer-version banner, so nothing is broken, but the ids in Tables 1–2 are the ones to
  use going forward.
